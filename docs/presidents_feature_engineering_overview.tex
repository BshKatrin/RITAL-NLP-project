        It then keeps speech order through metadata and can optionally append deterministic position features
        and local transition features before the BiLSTM.

\textbf{Feature block} &
\textbf{Concrete variables} &
\textbf{Added dim.} &
\textbf{Why it helps} &
\textbf{Status in repo} \\

Base sentence representation &
One dense sentence embedding $e_t \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$ for each sentence, loaded from the precomputed embedding matrix. &
$768$ &
Captures the lexical and semantic content of the sentence before any speech-level modeling. This is the core signal seen by every presidents model in the repo. &
\textcolor{deepgreen}{\textbf{Core}}\\

Speech structure metadata &
\texttt{speech\_id} and \texttt{sentence\_id}, used to rebuild full speeches and preserve sentence order. &
-- &
This is not concatenated to the vector, but it is essential: it lets the code batch whole speeches, keep chronological order, and run a sequence model instead of independent sentence classification. &
\textcolor{slate}{\textbf{Structural}}\\

Position features &
$p_t,\; 1-p_t,\; p_t^2,\; (1-p_t)^2,\; 2p_t-1,\; 1/L$ where $p_t$ is the normalized sentence position and $L$ is speech length. &
$+6$ &
Injects simple but useful priors: beginning vs.\ end of speech, centered location, non-linear position effects, and an explicit cue about overall speech length. &
\textcolor{amber}{\textbf{Optional}} in the full sequence pipeline\\

Transition features &
$\cos(e_t, e_{t-1})$, $\cos(e_t, e_{t+1})$, $\lVert e_t-e_{t-1}\rVert_2$, $\lVert e_{t+1}-e_t\rVert_2$ &
$+4$ &
Acts as a local change detector. Sharp embedding changes can reveal sentence boundaries where the topic, rhetoric, or speaker behavior shifts. Smooth neighborhoods look different from abrupt transitions. &
\textcolor{amber}{\textbf{Optional}} in the full sequence pipeline\\

Feature concatenation rule &
The final input is formed by concatenating the base embedding with any enabled optional blocks. &
varies &
Keeps the engineering simple and explainable: every extra block is deterministic, compact, and interpretable, so the BiLSTM still receives one feature vector per sentence. &
\textcolor{deepgreen}{\textbf{Current design}}\\

\normalsize
\noindent\textbf{Implementation note.}
The simplified training pipeline in the repo deliberately disables position and transition features to stay easier to explain,
while the fuller sequence pipeline can enable them as optional feature blocks.
